This update arrives through the app: click **Update now** when the banner appears.

## New

- **Compare maps:** when you filter the map, each map button shows how many of your selected markers that map has. Filters carry over as you switch maps, and a map with none of them says so.
- **Quests fill themselves in:** tracking the quests you have active shows how many earlier quests that proves you've finished, with one button to mark them done.

- **Find an item on the map:** on an item page, clicking a map under Found in opens it showing just where that item can be, with exits for planning the way out. It doesn't change your normal map filters.

## Fixed

- **Still Needed:** tracking an item that can't be crafted no longer adds one more on top of what a project or quest already needs.
- **Maps:** each map frames correctly when it opens, including when nothing is showing.
