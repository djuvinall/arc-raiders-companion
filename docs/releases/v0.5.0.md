This update arrives through the app: click **Update now** when the banner appears.

## New

- **ARC bestiary:** each ARC page now has a threat level, a written strategy built from its weak points, armor and attacks, guns that punch through heavy armor, and the maps it spawns on, each linking to that map. The wiki's tips appear as field notes.

## Data

- **A full data check:** every quest, item, recipe and reward was compared across MetaForge, the ARC Raiders Wiki and RaidTheory. 14 quest rewards that were missing are now listed, such as the Rascal from Boarding the Frigate and the Aphelion from Furtive Meetings.
- **Tian Wen** is spelled as in the game, a few duplicate cosmetics are gone, and ARC abilities no longer show stray markup.
- **The dataset has its own home:** it now lives at [arc-raiders-data](https://github.com/djuvinall/arc-raiders-data) under CC BY-SA 4.0, with the full audit. The app gets its data updates from there.
