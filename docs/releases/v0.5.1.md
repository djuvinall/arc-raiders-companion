This update arrives through the app: click **Update now** when the banner appears.

## New

- **Take back project resources:** an Undo appears right after you commit, **Take back** returns any amount to your stash, and **Back to stage** reopens a stage that finished by mistake. Stopping tracking now asks before it clears your progress.
- **More things can be undone:** importing a profile, Reset or Stop tracking on a quest, **Mark them done** and **Done, with all before it** all offer an Undo. Deleting a saved skill plan, a weapon build, a friend or a stencil screenshot asks for a second click, and **New** on Skills asks before it discards an unsaved plan.

## Fixed

- Esc no longer leaves the page while you're typing in a field.
- Clearing the quantity on Craft, the expedition bonus on Skills, or the target HP on Weapons no longer breaks the page.
- A fast double click can no longer commit twice or add a skill point past the maximum.
- Switching maps quickly no longer shows the previous map's markers, and the Raid plan no longer waits on the events feed.
- A second build of the same gun gets its own name, and saving says whether it updated an existing build.
- A failed app update can be retried.
- Quest names keep their real capitalisation, and workbench costs read "✓ 20/20" once you have enough.
- Buttons and list rows respond on hover, and the file pickers work from the keyboard.
