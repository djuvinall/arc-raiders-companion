This update arrives through the app: click **Update now** when the banner appears.

## For mid-raid

- **Keep, sell or recycle:** item pages give a one-line answer based on your goals and what the item is worth.
- **Quick search:** Ctrl+K finds items, quests, maps, ARCs and projects from any page.
- **Compact window:** an always-on-top window with item lookup, what you still need, and your active quests. Open it from the bottom bar.
- **ARC guide:** health, armor, weak points, attacks, tips and drops.

## For planning

- **Raid plan:** one page with the best map, events, quests, stops and needed gear.
- **Quest pins:** tracked quests show on the map.
- **Event-aware ranking:** when maps tie, the one with an event running comes first.
- **Workbench tracker:** set your levels on the Profile page, track the next upgrade, and see which recipes are out of reach.
- **Item sorting:** by value, value per kg or weight.

## Fixed

- **Codex:** the Trigger Nade blueprint was listed twice, so blueprints now total 91, matching the game.

## Improved

- **Stash:** edit counts from item pages and the Still Needed list.
- **Upgrades:** choose the level you already own, so its cost isn't counted.
- **Quests:** mark a quest and everything before it done in one click.
- **Layout:** item names show on every tile, item pages no longer scroll inside a panel, and the skill tree fills the page.
