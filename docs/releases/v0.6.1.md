This update arrives through the app: click **Update now** when the banner appears.

## New

- **Raid plans.** The Raid page is where you plan a raid now. Since you don't know your spawn, a plan picks **target areas** worth heading to, each with how much of what you want has good chances there, a short loop of stops ending near an exit, and the nearest exits. Switch off whole categories (quests, tracked crafts, projects, workbenches, items you add) or single items and container types. Or make **your own stops** by clicking the map, or by copying an area's loop. **Start raid** shows the stops as a checklist in the Compact Window.
- **Farm finder.** Pick one or more items and the Farm tab ranks the best areas across every map. **Plan this** turns one into a raid plan, and item pages link to it from **Best places to farm it**.
- **Home** is one card per tab: your raid plans, live events, each quest's next step, projects, ready now, still needed, workbenches, your starred skill plan, the Codex and the latest patch. Each card links straight in, and cards with nothing to show stay hidden.
- **Project stages played as missions.** Soul's Offering stage 4, Honor The Fallen, showed an empty page. It now lists its steps like a quest, and ticking the last one finishes the stage.

## Changed

- **Item cards** are closer to the in-game tooltip: the game's plain heavy font for names and tags, a larger description, weapon mod slots as greyed tiles, the ammo icon on Ammo Type, and no tier tag or flavor line.
- **Maps** no longer plans routes. It has **Plan a raid here** and lists the plans you've made for that map.

Chances in raid plans are a model built from container types, the loot areas around them and item rarity; no source publishes drop rates. Distances are straight lines.
