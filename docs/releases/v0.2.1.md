This update arrives through the app: click **Update now** when the banner appears.

## Fixed

- **Maps:** Spaceport and Blue Gate were showing only their underground layer, and Stella Montis only its bottom floor. All three now have a floor switcher, and markers follow the floor you pick.
- **Gun levels:** each level's upgrade modifiers now show, such as 25.7% Increased Fire Rate. Stat DPS and comparisons include the fire rate bonus.
- **Skills:** every Mobility max rank is confirmed from the game. Right Back At It takes a single point.

## New

- **Needed quests:** tracking a quest marks the unfinished quests before it as Needed, and shows which tracked quest each one is for.
- **Friends:** swap share codes on the Quests page to see what you can do together, who needs to catch up on what, and the best map to play.
