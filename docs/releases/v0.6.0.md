This update arrives through the app: click **Update now** when the banner appears.

## New

- **Weapons has two tabs.** **Compare** puts up to four builds side by side, and you can add one from your saved builds. **Saved builds** shows each build as a card. New charts compare time to kill through no shield, Light, Medium and Heavy shields (body or headshots, shots or seconds), plus damage, fire rate, headshot multiplier and damage per second.
- **Item cards look like the in-game tooltip:** rarity tags, stat rows, weapon stat bars, augment slots, what a mod fits and does, what an item recycles into, and weight and value along the bottom.
- **Quests nest like the in-game log.** Steps you can do in any order sit in a box whose checkbox ticks itself, "complete either or" quests such as To The Skies finish when one option is done, and optional steps no longer hold a quest back. The same view is in the Compact Window, where each quest now collapses.
- **Keys show the doors they open**, with a link to each one on the map.
- **Where to find:** items no source places, such as Insulation Rolls, now say where to look.
- **Skills:** star a plan to open it every time, and let a plan save itself when you leave the page.
- **Workbenches:** raising a level on Profile takes its materials from your stash, and lowering it gives them back. A switch turns this off for levels you reached before using the app.

## Changed

- Weapon fire rate in RPM, headshot multipliers and shield numbers now come from the wiki. The Heavy Shield's charge is 90, as it has been since patch 2.3.0.
- The dataset changelog keeps one line per file and says which game patch each update was checked against.
