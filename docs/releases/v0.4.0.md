This update arrives through the app: click **Update now** when the banner appears.

## New

- **Home:** the app now opens on a summary of your quests, needed items, blueprints and workshop, with what's Ready, what's Almost there, and what's happening right now.
- **Outpost tracking:** set your Outpost level on the Profile page and track its next room. Benches that need a higher Outpost say so.
- **Blueprint screenshot import:** paste or pick a screenshot of the game's Blueprints page, review what it found, and apply it. It runs on your PC, and nothing is uploaded.
- **Maps:** every marker MetaForge has mapped, about 43,000 across the seven maps, with an icon for each type, labels for named places, and a legend that shows and hides each type and remembers your choice. Drawing is faster too.
