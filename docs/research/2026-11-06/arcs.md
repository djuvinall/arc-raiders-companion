## Existing tips that look stale or wrong

1. **Bastion: "Deadline can instantly defeat a Bastion when deployed near the canister." DISPUTED or STALE.** Deadline was added in 1.2.0 (2025-11-13). One week later 1.3.0 (2025-11-20) cut explosive damage against large ARC "like the Bastion" and fixed explosions hitting several parts at once. The PC Gamer one-shot article dates from the launch period. EZG (Mar/Sep 2026) warns a poorly placed mine leaves it alive with much of its health, while a 2026 guide says 1 to 2 Deadlines. Rewrite as: "A Deadline planted at the core or canister can kill it or nearly kill it; bring a second." 1.7.0 also stopped Deadline damage passing through walls.
2. **Bombardier: "one well-placed Deadline near its canister will destroy it in one mine." DISPUTED.** Same reasoning (1.3.0 explosive rebalance). No post-patch source confirms a guaranteed one-mine kill. EZG only says plant it on the rear chassis.
3. **Comet: "As of right now it seems to ALWAYS drop the igniter if destroyed properly." DISPUTED.** allthings.how (2026-02-25) says the drop is not guaranteed and players report missing Igniters after clean kills. KeenGamer (2026-02-26) says it should always spawn barring glitches. Drop "ALWAYS". The shield-damage figures ("about 1/4 shield remaining") have no source beyond the wiki; allthings.how gives "around 50% health without a shield". Treat both as rough.
4. **Comet: "2–3 Seeker Grenades".** Seeker damage was inconsistent around 1.36.0 and was fixed in 1.39.0 (2026-07-28). The current count is unverified. Keep it as a soft suggestion.
5. **Hydra: "stand right in front in the middle between the two gun segments, the guns can't aim at you." STALE.** 2.2.0 (2026-10-20) fixed the Hydra struggling to hit close-range targets and raised its base damage. Remove the tip, or add that it may no longer work.
6. **Hydra wiki respawn claim (not in dataset tips, but on the wiki): "On the Frigate the Hydra respawns consistently." DISPUTED.** allthings.how (2026-10-13) and games.gg (2026-10-22) both say a destroyed Hydra stays down for the raid. Do not import it.
7. **Matriarch: "Dam Battleground - Electrical Substation for cover." POSSIBLY STALE.** 1.7.0 (2025-12-16) moved the Matriarch's Dam Battlegrounds spawn "to an area that better plays to her strengths". Spot tips written before December 2025 may no longer fit. The Spaceport and Blue Gate spots have no counter-evidence.
