# ARC Raiders: loot, stash and economy tips (research as of 2026-11-06, patch 2.4.1)

Format: tip | confidence | sources (URL + date)
Abbreviations: SN = Steam news (official Embark announcement, via https://api.steampowered.com/ISteamNews/GetNewsForApp/v2/?appid=1808500). Wiki = arcraiders.wiki, with the page's last-revision date.
Big context: Frozen Trail 2.0 (2026-10-08) retired Cred, replaced Raider Decks with Reward Passes, added the Outpost and Research Station, replaced Ermal with Cassio, and raised the stash cap. Many pre-October guides are STALE on these points.

## Keep vs recycle vs sell
- Don't sell or recycle quest items before the quest is done. Embark lists it as a known issue: losing or selling one (example: Equatorial Sundial for "Clean Hands") can make the quest impossible to complete. | CONFIRMED (official) | SN Store Update 2.2.0 (2026-10-20); SN Live Update 2.3.0 (2026-10-27)

Mechanical Components, 4 Sentinel Firing Core. Gear Bench 2: 3 Power Cable, 5 Electrical Components, 5 Hornet Driver. Gear Bench 3: 3 Industrial Battery, 5 Adv. Electrical Components, 6 Bastion Cell. Medical Lab 1: 50 Fabric, 6 ARC Alloy. Medical Lab 2: 2 Cracked Bioscanner, 5 Durable Cloth, 8 Tick Pod. Medical Lab 3: 3 Rusted Shut Medical Kit, 8 Antiseptic, 5 Surveyor Vault. Explosives 1: 50 Chemicals, 6 ARC Alloy. Explosives 2: 3 Synthesized Fuel, 5 Crude Explosives, 5 Pop Trigger. Explosives 3: 3 Laboratory Reagents, 5 Explosive Compound, 3 Rocketeer Driver. Refiner 1: 60 Metal Parts, 5 ARC Powercell. Refiner 2: 3 Toaster, 5 ARC Motion Core, 8 Fireball Burner. Refiner 3: 3 Motor, 10 ARC Circuitry, 6 Bombardier Cell. | CONFIRMED (two sources agree; no patch note changes these) | https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Workshop (rev 2026-10-31); https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/arc-raiders-all-workshop-scrappy-and-expedition-upgrades-guide (2025-11-05)

- Frozen Trail made several "recyclable-looking" items useful. Planks, Sheet Metal, Steel Cable, Epoxy Bucket, Hand Drill, Laser Level, Insulation Roll and Cable Stripper go to Outpost builds and furniture. - Don't sell unlearned blueprints. Learned duplicates sell for 5,000 Coins (or give them to a friend). | CONFIRMED (wiki + Dexerto + Timesaver agree) | https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Blueprints (rev 2026-11-01); https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/arc-raiders/blueprints/ (2026-01-22); https://timesaver.gg/blog/arc-raiders-best-items-to-sell (updated 2026-10-08)

## Recycling vs salvaging in raid
- Recycling (in Speranza) gives the full yield. Salvaging in raid gives less, roughly half (guides' examples range about 50-57%). Carry valuables home and only salvage to free weight. | CONFIRMED (multiple independent guides agree on "less"; the exact ratio varies by item) | https://arcraidershub.com/needed-items-recycling-guide (2026-10-09); https://allthings.how/arc-raiders-recycling-recycle-salvage-and-sell-items-efficiently/ (date n/a); https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Loot (rev 2026-04-14)
- Item tooltips show recycle and salvage outputs as icons, so check before breaking something down. | CONFIRMED (official) | SN 1.17.0 (2026-02-24)
- In-raid salvaging is now a skill ("Field Salvaging") in the reworked 2.0 skill tree. | LIKELY (named in official bug-fix notes; how it works not documented) | SN Store Update 2.1.0 (2026-10-13)
- The Acquire Resources menu prefers crafting suggestions over recycle suggestions. | CONFIRMED (official) | SN 1.26.0 (2026-04-28)

## Scrappy (rooster)
- Scrappy collects Metal Parts, Fabric, Plastic Parts, Chemicals, Rubber Parts and Assorted Seeds only while you are Topside, not while offline or idle in Speranza. Payout scales with your own time in the round: 1+, 6+ and 15+ minute tiers, capped at 15 minutes. | LIKELY (wiki detail; guides agree in general) | https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Scrappy (rev 2026-11-06)
- Collect from Scrappy regularly. He fills up after 5 rounds and stops gathering until you empty him. | LIKELY (wiki) | https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Scrappy (rev 2026-11-06)
- Upgrade costs: L2 1 Dog Collar, L3 3 Apricot + 3 Lemon, L4 6 Olives + 6 Prickly Pear + 1 Cat Bed, L5 12 Apricot + 12 Mushroom + 3 Very Comfortable Pillow. | CONFIRMED (wiki + Gfinity agree) | https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Scrappy (rev 2026-11-06); https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/arc-raiders-all-workshop-scrappy-and-expedition-upgrades-guide (2025-11-05)
- Whether Scrappy gives MORE basic materials when you die than when you extract is disputed. The wiki table and some guides say yes (a pity payout). KeenGamer says failed raids give less. | DISPUTED | https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Scrappy (rev 2026-11-06); https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/arc-raiders-how-to-upgrade-scrappy-to-max-level/ (date n/a)
- Feeding: queue up to 6 foods. Each round of 6+ minutes eats one food, adds +5 of the linked material, and gives a bonus item from that food's pool (e.g. Apricot for gunsmith materials, Candleberries for Research Station items, added in 2.2.0). Feeding was added in 1.22.0. | CONFIRMED (official notes confirm the feature; wiki gives the details) | SN Flashpoint 1.22.0 (2026-03-31); https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Scrappy (rev 2026-11-06)
- Expedition streaks boost Scrappy's materials by 6% per consecutive Expedition, capped at 3 levels (18%). The buff persists during the current Expedition pause. | CONFIRMED (official) | SN Preparing for your Second Expedition (2026-02-05); SN The 5th Expedition (2026-08-21)

## Blueprints
- You learn a blueprint in Speranza ("Learn and Consume"), not in raid. An unlearned blueprint is ordinary loot and is lost if you die. Put it in your safe pocket. | CONFIRMED (wiki + Dexerto + allthings.how agree) | https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Blueprints (rev 2026-11-01); https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/arc-raiders/blueprints/ (2026-01-22); https://allthings.how/arc-raiders-blueprints-find-drops-fast-and-claim-them-safely (date n/a)
- Container type hints: explosives in industrial containers, weapons and gun parts in raider containers, meds in medical containers, augments in security containers, utilities in electrical containers. Rare and epic blueprints are more common during major map conditions. | LIKELY (wiki; spawns flagged as not 100% confirmed) | https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Blueprints (rev 2026-11-01)
- Dolabra, Bobcat, Equalizer, Jupiter, Tempest and Vulcano blueprints can now drop in any major map condition. | CONFIRMED (official) | SN Live Update 2.3.0 (2026-10-27)
- The Emperor Key blueprint drops less often from Redirection pods, with ARC parts and high-tier ARC loot added to compensate. | CONFIRMED (official) | SN 2.3.0 (2026-10-27)
- First Wave Caches (Hurricane) give slightly fewer blueprints since 1.18.0, but better high-tier materials. | CONFIRMED (official) | SN 1.18.0 (2026-03-03)
- Since 2.0 the Outpost's Research Station can unlock many blueprints with Research Points, earned by extracting with special Research Items and choosing "Study". Research also costs materials (e.g. Yank Grenade blueprint: 1,000 RP + 8 Steel Cable). Not every blueprint is researchable. | LIKELY (multiple guides agree on the mechanism; official notes only confirm the station exists) | https://arcraiders.com/news/frozen-trail-2-0-update (2026-10-08); https://timesaver.gg/blog/arc-raiders-research-points-what-to-unlock-first (2026-10-12); https://arcraidershub.com/needed-items-recycling-guide (2026-10-09); https://senseos.gg/guides/arc-raiders/how-to-get-blueprints/ (2026-10-27)
- Weekly Trials can award blueprints (random uncommon, rare and epic item-or-blueprint rewards at 1,000, 2,000 and 3,000 points per challenge). | LIKELY (wiki) | https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Trials (rev 2026-10-27)
- Shani no longer sells blueprints (her Cred blueprint offers ended with 2.0). | LIKELY (wiki) | https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Shani (rev 2026-10-12)
- Furniture Designs and Weapon Stencils work like blueprints: learn once, then craft or apply. There are 100 designs: 73 found Topside, 22 from Outpost upgrades, 4 from the pass, 1 DLC. | CONFIRMED (official) | https://arcraiders.com/news/frozen-trail-2-0-update (2026-10-08)

## Safe pocket / augment safe slots
- Items in the safe pocket survive death and go to your stash. Everything else is lost. | CONFIRMED (wiki + allthings.how) | https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Loadout (rev 2026-06-21); https://allthings.how/arc-raiders-safe-pockets-increase-slots-and-keep-loot-on-death (2025-11-08)
- Safe pocket slots by augment: all Mk.1 augments 1. Looting Mk.2 2. Looting Mk.3 Cautious 2. Looting Mk.3 Survivor 3. Tactical Mk.3 Healing 3. Tactical Mk.3 Revival 2. Tactical Mk.3 Smoke 2. Combat Mk.3 Flanking 2. Combat Mk.2, Mk.3 Aggressive and Tactical Mk.2 1. Safekeeper 1. Free Loadout 0. | LIKELY (wiki table; older guides say "Looting Mk.2 Epic = 3", which looks stale) | https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Augments (rev 2026-03-18)
- Weapons can't go in the safe pocket, except with Looting Mk.3 (Safekeeper), whose safe pocket takes any item. Embark patched exploits that let weapons in without it. | CONFIRMED (official + wiki) | SN 1.18.0 (2026-03-03); SN 1.19.0 (2026-03-10); https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Augments (rev 2026-03-18)
- Going in with no loadout at all still gives 10 backpack slots and 1 safe pocket. That beats a free loadout (which has no safe pocket) for a targeted pickup. | LIKELY (wiki) | https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Loadout (rev 2026-06-21)
- Keys stay in the safe pocket when you use the Unload backpack. | CONFIRMED (official) | SN Cold Snap 1.7.0 (2025-12-16)

## Free loadouts
- A free loadout is a random low-tier kit (Kettle, Stitcher, Rattler or Ferro, plus Light Shield, ammo, meds and a utility) on the Free Loadout Augment: 14 slots, 4 quick-use, max weight 35, no safe pocket. | CONFIRMED (wiki + allthings.how) | https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Loadout (rev 2026-06-21); https://allthings.how/arc-raiders-safe-pockets-increase-slots-and-keep-loot-on-death (2025-11-08)
- Some map conditions (Night Raid, Close Scrutiny since 1.33.0, possibly more later) ban free loadouts. | CONFIRMED (official) | SN Live Update 1.33.0 (2026-06-16); SN 1.36.0 (2026-07-07)
- Matchmaking puts custom-loadout players into fresh servers more often. Free loadouts more often join mid-round. | CONFIRMED (official dev note via wiki) | https://arcraiders.com/news/patch-notes-1-22-0 (2026-03-31) cited by https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Loadout
- A free loadout needs stash room for your current custom loadout to move back. Free Loadout Augment trades work even with an overflowing stash. | LIKELY (wiki) / CONFIRMED for the trade part (SN 1.17.0) | https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Loadout (rev 2026-06-21); SN 1.17.0 (2026-02-24)

## Stash
- Max stash is now 328 slots (two new tiers in 2.0), not counting Expedition bonus slots or Envoy-bought slots. | CONFIRMED (official) | SN FROZEN TRAIL 2.0 (2026-10-08); https://arcraiders.com/news/frozen-trail-2-0-update
- Stash upgrades cost Coins, +24 slots each, from a base of 64. Cumulative cost: 280 slots = 675k, 304 = 1.425M, 328 = 2.925M. The last two tiers cost 750k and 1.5M on their own. | LIKELY (wiki only) | https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Speranza (rev 2026-10-10)
- Expeditions give +12 stash per completion, capped at +60 after the 5th. These slots are permanent. | CONFIRMED (official) | SN The Fourth Expedition (2026-06-25); SN The 5th Expedition (2026-08-21)

## Expeditions (wipe)

- The Expedition Vault was removed in 2.3.0. Vault items moved to your stash, and purchased slots were refunded as Bronze Statuettes. Partly built Caravans were refunded as vouchers/statuettes usable with Cassio or sellable. | CONFIRMED (official) | SN Live Update 2.3.0 (2026-10-27); SN The 5th Expedition (2026-08-21)

You keep cosmetics, Raider Tokens, Trials and leaderboards, Codex entries, unlocked maps, event progress, and bonus skill points and stash slots from Expeditions. | CONFIRMED (official notes list what's kept; wiki lists what resets; the "temporary blueprints" note implies blueprints reset) | SN Preparing for your Second Expedition (2026-02-05); SN The Fourth Expedition (2026-06-25); SN 1.45.0 (2026-09-08); https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Expedition (rev 2026-10-08)
- Rules for the 2027 return are unannounced. Don't ship old stash-value thresholds (e.g. 3M Coins for 5 skill points) as current. | STALE (pre-pause rules) | SN Preparing for your Second Expedition (2026-02-05)

## Currencies: Cred, Raider Tokens, Reward Pass
- Cred is gone. It expired with Frozen Trail (2026-10-08) and was replaced by Reward Points, which feed the active Reward Pass. Guides mentioning Cred or Raider Decks are stale. | CONFIRMED (official) | SN Live Update 1.45.0 (2026-09-08); https://arcraiders.com/news/frozen-trail-2-0-update (2026-10-08)
- Only one Reward Pass is active at a time. Old Raider Deck rewards live in the free Legacy Pass, and past claims are kept. | CONFIRMED (official) | https://arcraiders.com/news/frozen-trail-2-0-update (2026-10-08)
- The Frozen Trail Pass has 60 levels. The free track includes gameplay items and up to 200 Raider Tokens. The premium track costs 1,150 Tokens, is cosmetic-only, and pays back up to 1,150 Tokens. | CONFIRMED (official) | SN Frozen Trail (2026-10-08)
- Reward Points come from Feats: Easy 15, Medium 25, Hard 50. Weekly milestones give 250 each at 4, 8 and 12 progress (Easy +1, Medium +2, Hard +3). After all 3 milestones, Feats still give points but no milestone rewards until the weekly reset. Daily Feats can be rerolled (up to 3 times per the wiki). | CONFIRMED (official numbers) / LIKELY (reroll count) | https://arcraiders.com/news/frozen-trail-2-0-update (2026-10-08); https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Feats (rev 2026-10-23)
- Raider Tokens (premium currency) can also be earned from passes, projects, events, Expedition objectives (150 each in Expedition 4) and Cassio offers. | CONFIRMED (official) | SN The Fourth Expedition (2026-06-25); SN Frozen Trail (2026-10-08); https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Nomadic_Envoys (rev 2026-10-22)

## Weekly Trials
- Trials unlock at level 15. There are 5 challenges per week. Your best single-run score per challenge counts, and it is tallied when you extract. Season 6 began 2026-10-08. | LIKELY (wiki; Season 6 start matches the official note that Trials resumed Oct 8) | https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Trials (rev 2026-10-27); SN The 5th Expedition (2026-08-21)
- Per-challenge rewards come at 1,000, 2,000 and 3,000 points (random uncommon, rare, epic item or blueprint), claimable once per tier per week. Season-end rank rewards are cosmetics. | LIKELY (wiki) | https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Trials (rev 2026-10-27)
- Ranks run in 100-player divisions with weekly promotion and demotion. A new season starts you 2 ranks below your last placement. The top 1,000 Hotshots become Cantina Legend. | LIKELY (wiki) | https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Trials (rev 2026-10-27)
- Trial objectives show bonus conditions (e.g. a map condition), and the final rank is worldwide. | CONFIRMED (official) | SN Cold Snap 1.7.0 (2025-12-16)
